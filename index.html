Lineage & Philosophy

Keeping the sacred spirit of Braj alive.

In the villages of Mathura, Govardhan, and Vrindavan, traditional crafts have sustained families for generations. As synthetic factory imports flooded local markets, delicate handmade practices like zari embellishment, cow dung molding, and folk painting faced severe decline.

Braj Surbhi was created as an institutional initiative under Khajani Welfare Society to revive these sacred arts. By organizing women into village-level cottage self-help groups, we provide raw materials, specialized design workshops, and direct market access.

Every piece ordered through our coordination desk directly supports independent craft families, ensuring traditional skills thrive in the next generation.

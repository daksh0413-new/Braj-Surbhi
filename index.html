About Braj Surbhi

Rooted in Braj Bhoomi, dedicated to tradition and community

Our Vision

Braj Surbhi was formed to serve as a cultural and artisanal bridge. Braj Bhoomi has given centuries of devotion, music, craftsmanship, and auspicious art to the world. We work diligently to preserve this holy ethos by encouraging local communities to produce traditional handicraft items with modern quality standards.

As an initiative nurtured under the guidance of Khajani Welfare Society, our programs emphasize community self-reliance, preserving vanishing folk patterns, and connecting artisans directly with worshippers, patrons, and cultural enthusiasts.

Institutional Roots

Khajani Welfare Society spearheads community training, women's self-help collectives, and skill enablement across key districts.

Braj Surbhi operates as the cultural outreach wing representing these traditional artisans.
